# Robo-Advisers

## What they are
Robo-advisers are automated, digital investment advisory programs. You answer an online questionnaire
about your goals, time horizon, income, assets, and risk tolerance; an algorithm then recommends and
manages a portfolio — usually of low-cost index funds or ETFs — and rebalances it automatically.

## Potential benefits
- Lower fees and account minimums than many traditional advisers.
- Automatic diversification and rebalancing.
- Some offer access to a human professional ("hybrid" advice).

## What to understand before signing up
- **Limited questionnaire:** a few questions may not capture your full financial situation — other
  accounts, debts, or upcoming expenses.
- **How the algorithm works:** what assumptions it makes, how often it rebalances, and whether it
  favors the provider's own products.
- **Fees:** the advisory fee plus the expense ratios of the underlying funds.
- **Level of human interaction:** whether you can talk to a person, and when.
- **Registration:** robo-advisers are typically registered investment advisers; you can check them on
  Investor.gov.

## Takeaway
A robo-adviser automates a sound, diversified approach at low cost, but you are still responsible
for giving accurate information and making sure its recommendations fit your goals.
